## Interpretation

- Against MultiCam-style, Universal is better on: desert, jungle, mountain, snow, tundra.
- Against MultiCam-style, Universal is worse on: forest, taiga, urban.
- Against MTP-style, Universal is better on: desert, mountain, snow, tundra.
- Against MTP-style, Universal is worse on: forest, jungle, taiga, urban.
- Against Vegetata-style, Universal is better on: desert, mountain, snow, tundra, urban.
- Against Vegetata-style, Universal is worse on: forest, jungle, taiga.

These results compare fixed synthetic controls, not official commercial or military garments.
A proper product comparison would require legally sourced fabric samples and the same human test protocol.
